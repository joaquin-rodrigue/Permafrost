# Permafrost
Welcome to the tundra.

The snow is thick, the world is frozen over, and you've run out of gas. If you're gonna get out of this mess, you need to brave the cold and search what's left of this land for more.

This game is an open-world survival game that forces you to scavenge what's left of civilization to start your car and find greener pastures, with a mix of horror threats that have come to eat you.
Ok, game pitch over

## The World
This game uses Simplex Noise as the basis for its world generation algorithm. That, combined with many in-world features leads to a snowy, mountainous tundra with rather diverse environments.
Along with the natural world, there are procedurally generated structures (WIP) to be found occasionally in the wild, which serve as the main source of important items to the player as well as identifiable landmarks. Due to the thick forest throughout the map, players have to learn to navigate by landmarks, or by making their own markers (WIP? sorta?).

## The Creatures
This game has some creatures ranging from simple animals roaming the forest, to cryptid monsters that stalk and hunt the player. Each major threat poses different challenges for the players that face them.
All creatures (will eventually) have complex behavior that makes them feel more alive. Hunting down a small animal for food may prove somewhat challenging, meanwhile wolves might gather into a pack to attack the player. Large cryptids offer more specific challenges when encountered, and the player must work around the threats they pose.
